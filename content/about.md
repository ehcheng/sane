# SANE: School Alerts, Notices & Essentials

## How It Works

Connect your school Gmail and tell SANE about your kids. AI reads every incoming email, picks out what actually matters, and delivers clear summaries to you and your family.

- **AI-powered filtering.** Events, deadlines, schedule changes, and action items are extracted automatically. Everything else is quietly set aside.
- **Forwarding and briefs.** Get summaries forwarded as they arrive, or opt for a daily or weekly digest. Send to your partner, the grandparents, anyone who needs to know.
- **Calendar sync.** School events and deadlines are extracted and added to a calendar you can subscribe to from Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook.
- **Events dashboard.** See everything coming up in one place, labeled by child, with agenda and month views.
- **You stay in control.** Review AI decisions, give feedback, set sender rules, and pause or disconnect at any time.

## Privacy First

Your data stays yours. Children's details, forwarding addresses, and Gmail credentials are all encrypted at rest. We never sell your data, serve ads, or use your emails to train AI models. Read our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for full details.
